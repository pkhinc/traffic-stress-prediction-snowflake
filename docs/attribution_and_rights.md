# Attribution, rights and public repository scope

## Academic context

This portfolio repository is based on **“Traffic Stress Index – Analiza czynników wpływających na stres kierowców”**, an academic **group project completed in May 2026** at Gdańsk University of Technology. The original thesis lists **four student co-authors**.

The public portion is intentionally limited to the **individual modelling notebook supplied by the portfolio owner**, the **Streamlit application supplied by the owner** (with a documented input-schema adjustment) and illustrative screenshots. It **does not claim individual authorship of the complete database, original preprocessing pipeline, EDA, dataset or whole academic report**.

## Materials intentionally omitted

- The full joint diploma thesis (`.docx`/PDF): not published pending confirmation of all co-authors' permissions, the university's first-publication period and any other applicable conditions.
- The 50,000-row CSV dataset: source/license/permission not established.
- Snowflake `.pkl` model and preprocessor artifacts: not provided; the preprocessor was produced within group work, and permission to redistribute it has not been established.
- Secrets, credentials, account-specific exports and personal information from the original document.
